## Key Callouts

**🎯 Most Needs Distribution Push**: **Salmon & Sweet Potato 15lb** with only 60.1% ACV distribution compared to 88.1% and 91.9% for the Chicken & Rice SKUs. Despite lower distribution, it already captures 24.1% category share.

**💰 Best Return on Promo Spend**: **Salmon & Sweet Potato 15lb** delivers 16.9% promo lift, generating $24,445.42 in incremental dollars—the highest incremental return despite having only 47.7% of its volume on promotion. This is 3.2x the lift of Chicken & Rice 15lb (5.2%) and 2.8x the lift of Chicken & Rice 4lb (6.0%).

## Summary

The portfolio shows healthy fundamentals with total category sales of $702,170.61 across the 9-week period. Chicken & Rice 15lb leads in category share (44.6%) and has near-optimal distribution (88.1%). Chicken & Rice 4lb achieves the highest distribution reach (91.9%). However, the standout opportunity lies in Salmon & Sweet Potato 15lb, which combines strong distribution momentum, superior promotional responsiveness, and significant room for expanded retail presence.
